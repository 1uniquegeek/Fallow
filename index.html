A different kind of wellness company

We don't just tell people to be healthier. We teach them how.

Health is shaped by what we eat, how we move, how we manage stress, where we live, who supports us, and whether we can access the resources we need.

The eight dimensions

Wellness is more than physical health.

Our programs consider the whole person and the environment around them.

PhysicalMovement, nutrition, sleep, preventive health.
EmotionalStress skills, self-awareness, support and healthy coping.
SocialConnection, belonging, family and community.
IntellectualLearning, curiosity, health literacy and skill building.
OccupationalPurpose, career development and meaningful work.
FinancialPractical money skills, stability and resource navigation.
EnvironmentalSustainability, gardens, food systems and healthy spaces.
SpiritualReflection, values, meaning, nature and personal practices.
